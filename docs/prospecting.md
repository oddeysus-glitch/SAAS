# Prospecting Platform

This is the sales layer for the whole system.

It is not just a landing page for two apps.
It is the place where you turn the system into revenue.

## How to think about it

You are not selling software.
You are selling results.

- Headshots: better photos for profiles
- Blog posts: publishable content without starting from blank

The prospecting platform exists to:
- find people who want those results
- show them the offer
- get them in
- let them preview
- get payment
- deliver the result

## The repeatable unit

Every product in this system follows the same shape:

1. One clear result
2. One free preview
3. One payment path
4. One account
5. One admin flow
6. One support path

That is the unit you scale.
Not a new stack per product.
The same stack, new offer.

## Who to prospect first

Start with people who already want the result and can pay for it.

For headshots:
- professionals
- job seekers
- real estate agents
- coaches
- consultants
- creators with a profile they care about

For blog posts:
- solo founders
- small business owners
- marketers
- creators
- agency owners
- anyone who needs content regularly

Pick one audience first.
Do not prospect everyone at once.

## The offer

A good prospecting offer is simple.

Example shape:
- try one free preview
- pay with PayPal or Venmo
- get the result
- keep the account for more later

Do not lead with features.
Lead with the result, the price, and the next step.

## Channels to use

Use the channels where your audience already is.

Good starting options:
- direct messages
- email
- short posts
- profile optimization
- community participation
- referrals
- direct outreach to a small list

Do not try to be everywhere.
Pick one or two channels and use them consistently.

## The prospecting loop

A simple loop:

1. Find a buyer.
2. Show the result they care about.
3. Offer a free preview.
4. Get them to try it.
5. If they like it, show the payment step.
6. Collect payment.
7. Deliver.
8. Ask for a referral or repeat use.

That is the business loop.
The app supports it.
The prospecting platform is the part that gets people into it.

## What to build for prospecting

You do not need a huge marketing site first.
You need a few things:

- a clear homepage
- a clear pricing page
- a clear billing page
- a clear result showcase
- a clear way to contact support
- a clear way for buyers to pay

Later, add:
- a results gallery
- before/after samples
- testimonials
- case examples
- a referral flow
- a simple outreach list

## What matters most early

Early, the prospecting platform is mostly about trust and clarity.

Buyers need to see:
- what they get
- what it costs
- how they pay
- what happens after they pay
- how to get help

If those are clear, the system can sell itself a lot better.

## Scaling plan

Once the first product sells:

1. Keep the first audience tight.
2. Improve the offer based on real buyers.
3. Add a second product from the same system.
4. Cross-sell into the same account.
5. Reuse the same admin and support flow.
6. Add more products as you find repeatable demand.

That is how this becomes a platform instead of one-off apps.

## Owner habits that matter

If you want this to pay bills, do these things consistently:

- talk to buyers
- watch what they ask for
- fix the friction they mention
- keep the offer simple
- keep payment easy
- deliver quickly
- follow up after delivery
- ask for referrals

The app is only half the system.
The other half is how you put it in front of people.
